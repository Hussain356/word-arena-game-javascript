How to play

Word Guess: pick letters (tap or type) before you run out of lives. Hints reveal a letter for −5 points.

Word Scramble: unscramble the letters before the timer ends. Faster answers score more.

Consecutive wins build a streak bonus (up to +25). Your best score is saved in this browser.
